## Super Mario: Coin Climb

A tiny big-leap platformer for phones, tablets, and desktop. Every one of the 40 coins makes Mario jump higher. Collect all of them and reach the castle flag.

### Controls

- **Touch:** on-screen left/right buttons plus the big **JUMP** button.
- **Keyboard:** `←` / `→` to run, `Space` (or `↑`) to jump.
- **Tilt:** optional motion steering. Toggle it on the start or pause screen. On iOS, motion access is requested inside the tap so Safari allows it.
- **Pause:** the pause button in the top-right, or `P` / `Esc`.
- **Sound:** toggle on the start or pause screen, or with the speaker button.

The jump meter shows your current jump-height multiplier. Grab every coin to reach **2.92×** and clear the course.
